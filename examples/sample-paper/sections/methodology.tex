\section{Methodology}
\label{sec:method}

Let $K$ be a bounded, compactly supported kernel and $h = h_n \to 0$ a
bandwidth sequence. The Nadaraya--Watson estimator is
\begin{equation}
  \hat m_h(x) = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n K\!\left(\frac{x - X_i}{h}\right) Y_i}
                     {\sum_{i=1}^n K\!\left(\frac{x - X_i}{h}\right)}.
  \label{eq:nw}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform consistency]
\label{thm:main}
Suppose $m$ is Lipschitz, $f$ is bounded away from zero on $[0,1]$, and
$nh/\log n \to \infty$. Then
\[
  \sup_{x \in [0,1]} \bigl|\hat m_h(x) - m(x)\bigr| \xrightarrow{\;p\;} 0 .
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The bias term is handled by the Lipschitz condition. For the stochastic
term, a standard chaining argument over a grid of mesh $h^2$ combined
with Bernstein's inequality yields the rate. We therefore conclude that
the estimator is consistent uniformly over the unit interval, which is
the sufficient condition required for the affine correction step in
Section~\ref{sec:conclusion}.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}
\label{lem:variance}
Under the assumptions of Theorem~\ref{thm:main}, the variance of the
stochastic term is of order $(nh)^{-1}$ uniformly in $x$.
\end{lemma}
