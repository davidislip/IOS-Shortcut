\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Nonparametric regression is a workhorse of modern statistics. The
difficulty of establishing uniform, rather than pointwise, rates is
well known; see \cite{gine2016mathematical} for an overview. Our goal is
a self-contained proof that avoids the usual entropy calculations.

Throughout, $X_1,\dots,X_n$ are i.i.d.\ with density $f$ on $[0,1]$ and
$Y_i = m(X_i) + \varepsilon_i$, where the errors $\varepsilon_i$ are
independent of the covariates and satisfy $\mathbb{E}\varepsilon_i = 0$
and $\mathbb{E}\varepsilon_i^2 = \sigma^2 < \infty$.
